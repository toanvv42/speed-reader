# A Quiet Reading Instrument

This sample exists to show how `speed-reader` feels on a real mixed document.

Press `Space` to play or pause. Press `p` to cycle presets. Press `c` to open the chapter picker when you want to jump ahead.

## Start Here

RSVP shows one word at a time. The point is not to race. The point is to stay with the sentence without your eyes scanning all over the page.

If you are new to this, start around `250` to `300` wpm. When the motion feels calm, move faster. When comprehension drops, move slower.

## Presets

`gentle` slows things down a bit and gives punctuation more breathing room.

`standard` is the default rhythm for everyday reading.

`technical` is better for code, specs, and dense structure.

`study` is the slowest and most deliberate mode.

## English

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. That sentence is useless as literature, but excellent for testing rhythm, punctuation, and word boundaries.

Longer paragraphs reveal more than isolated lines do. A good reading tool should make the page feel quieter, not merely faster.

## Tiếng Việt

Đây là một đoạn văn ngắn để kiểm tra cách ứng dụng xử lý tiếng Việt. Nếu nhịp đọc ổn định và từ ngữ vẫn dễ hiểu, trải nghiệm thường sẽ dễ chịu hơn nhiều.

Bạn có thể thử tăng tốc độ một chút, rồi giảm xuống khi gặp đoạn khó hơn. Mục tiêu là giữ được sự tập trung, không phải chạy đua với văn bản.

## Code

```rust
fn main() {
    let modes = ["gentle", "standard", "technical", "study"];
    for mode in modes {
        println!("preset: {mode}");
    }
}
```

## Punctuation

Short sentence. Longer one, with a comma; then a semicolon: and finally a period. Did that feel different? It should.
